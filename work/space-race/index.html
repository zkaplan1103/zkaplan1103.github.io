Space Race

A platformer where the world turns around you.

Play on itch.io ↗GitHub ↗

The problem

For our final we wanted one mechanic that changes how every level reads. We picked rotation. Hit certain points and the whole world turns, so a wall can become the floor.

How it works

01Run

Move and jump through a 2D level.

02Flip

Hit a trigger and the world rotates around the player.

03Portal

Portals send you across the map.

04Select

A level select lets you jump into any of the ten levels.

Details

I built it with a teammate in Unity and C#. Every level is designed around the rotation, so the same room plays differently once it turns.

This was our final project at DIS. I rebuilt it for WebGL in 2026 so it plays in the browser.
