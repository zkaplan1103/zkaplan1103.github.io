Space Race

A 2D platformer where the world rotates around you.

Play on itch.io ↗GitHub ↗

About the game

I made it with a teammate as our game development final at DIS. When you hit certain points the whole level turns, so a wall can become the floor.

What's in it

  • 10 levelsPlay them in order or pick one from the level select.
  • RotationHit a trigger and the world turns around the player.
  • PortalsStep in one and come out somewhere else on the map.

Making it

We wrote it in C# in Unity. I rebuilt it for WebGL in 2026 so it plays in the browser. The source is on GitHub.

  • Unity
