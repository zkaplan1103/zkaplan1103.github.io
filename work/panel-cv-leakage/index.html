Panel CV & Data Leakage

A popular cross validation tool was quietly training on the future.

GitHub ↗Paper ↗

The problem

On panel data the split itself can leak answers. The same movie can land in train and test. The model can also train on dates after the test window. Either way the score looks better than it should.

How it works

01Clean

Merged two sources into 254,062 daily rows for 3,400 movies and kept the group structure intact.

02Audit

Asserted on every fold. GroupTimeSeriesSplit was training on 2020 data against a 2006 test window.

03Fix

Wrote a splitter that sorts groups by date instead of by row order.

04Benchmark

Tuned linear regression, decision trees and XGBoost across four validation strategies.

Details

The community splitter orders groups by where they first appear in the array. It only works if the data was already sorted by date. Nothing warns you when it isn't.

Once both leaks are closed, test error rises and fold variance widens. That is the honest number. It's the one a forecast should use.

This was my Econ 167 final at Pomona. The repo includes a leakage checker that works on any splitter.
