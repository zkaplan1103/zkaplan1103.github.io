Panel CV & Data Leakage

I found a popular cross validation splitter training models on future data.

GitHub ↗Paper ↗

The question

For my Econ 167 final I wanted to see how much the choice of cross validation changes results on panel data. The data was daily box office numbers for 3,400 movies from 1999 to 2020.

What I found

The community GroupTimeSeriesSplit sorts groups by where they first show up in the data, not by date. If the data isn't already sorted by date, it trains on the future and still reports a good score.

I caught it because I checked every fold. One fold trained on data through 2020 and tested on 2006.

The fix

I wrote a version that takes the dates directly and sorts by them. I also wrote a checker that tests any splitter for both kinds of leakage.

Results

CV strategyWhat leaksXGBoost MSE
KFoldSame movie and future dates0.824
GroupKFoldFuture dates0.748
GroupTimeSeriesSplitSome future dates0.798
My fixed splitterNothing0.775

The leaky splits looked more stable than they should. Once both leaks are closed the error rises and varies more between folds. That number is less flattering, but it's the honest one to use for a forecast.
