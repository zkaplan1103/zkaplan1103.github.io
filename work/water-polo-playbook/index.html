Water Polo Playbook

An app I built for the team I coach to draw plays and watch them move.

Live app ↗GitHub ↗

Why I built it

Our plays lived on a whiteboard. Players saw them once in practice and forgot them. I wanted a place where the team could watch every play move step by step, on whatever device they had.

What it does

  • Boards to scaleHalf and full pool with the 2, 5 and 6 meter lines.
  • Formations6v6, 6on5, 5on6 and counterattack setups built in.
  • SlidesMove players slide by slide and the app animates between them.
  • Present modeLoops a play fullscreen on a TV or an iPad.
  • Lines and notesPass lanes, shot lines and notes on any slide.
  • PowerPoint exportEditable shapes with speaker notes.

How we use it

We build new plays with the head coach and walk the team through them in present mode. Everything saves on the device, so there's no account to set up.

Built with

  • Expo
