Water Polo Playbook

Plays that move, built for the team I coach.

Live app ↗GitHub ↗

The problem

Our plays lived on a whiteboard. Players saw them once and forgot them. I wanted one place where the team could watch every play move step by step.

How it works

01Set up

Pick a half or full pool board drawn to scale and a formation like 6v6 or 6on5.

02Build

Move players slide by slide. Add pass lanes, shot lines and notes.

03Play

The app animates every cap and the ball between slides.

04Present

Present mode loops the play fullscreen on a TV or an iPad.

Results

3platforms
4sample plays built in
0accounts or servers
.pptxexport with speaker notes

Details

Plays save on the device. There is no account or server. A play exports to PowerPoint with editable shapes and speaker notes, so a coach can drop it into a team meeting.

I use it with the boys team at The Hill School. We build new plays with the head coach and run them in practice from the app.

Folders, undo, light and dark themes and drag and drop are all in. Next is sharing a play by link.
