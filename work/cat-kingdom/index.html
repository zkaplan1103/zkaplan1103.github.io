Cat Kingdom

Four cat heroes. Swap between them to clear each level.

Play on itch.io ↗

The problem

Our game development midterm at DIS asked for a full 2D platformer. We wanted the puzzle to be picking the right character, not just jumping well.

How it works

01Story

A story intro sets up the kingdom before the first level.

02Swap

Switch between four cats mid level. One rolls, one attacks, one double jumps and one flies.

03Clear

Three levels built so each section needs the right cat at the right time.

04Finish

A final scene closes out the story.

Results

4playable cats
3levels
2person team
0downloads needed

Details

I built it with a teammate in Unity and C#. We wrote the character swap, movement and level logic ourselves. The art comes from third party sprite packs.

I rebuilt it for WebGL in 2026 so it plays in the browser with no download.
